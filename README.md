A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927) bounty entry: a short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay. The poem finds the bravery in not reaching: a hand on the latch, a door kept ajar, and the choice to remain in the room you're standing in.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, no dependencies) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`: stopping is not quitting, it is choosing where you stand — and finding that your own two feet are the only ground you know.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The program creates `/output` if needed and writes exactly the poem, nothing else. The poem is under 280 characters, as the competition requires.
